# LearnMath

LearnMath is an app I am building for studying graduate mathematics. You describe a topic, and it writes lecture notes with a language model and reads them aloud as a lecture, showing the text as it is spoken. It can also plan a whole course.

It runs in the browser, on a laptop or desktop computer (Chrome or Edge work best).

[Open LearnMath](/learnmath/)

Some things to know before using it:

* It needs your own [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/keys) API key, which pays for the language model. The key and your lessons are stored only in your browser.
* The lecture voice runs in your browser. It is downloaded the first time a lecture is recorded (between 90 and 330 MB).
* The notes are written by an AI model and may contain errors, so check important results against a textbook.
* It is a work in progress.
